Platform support

Every feature runs on Linux, macOS and Windows. Where the operating system does not provide something, portop leaves the field empty or shows ? instead of failing.

Targets

Linux on amd64, arm64, ARMv7 and ARMv6 with hardware floating point; macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon; Windows 10 1809 or later and Windows 11 on x64 and ARM64. ARMv5, soft-float armel, big-endian ARM, 32-bit x86 and 32-bit Windows are not supported. The validation report lists what has been tested on real hardware.
